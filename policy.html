2Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Uniplug, we may collect different types of information.

Account Information may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Password and login information
  • Profile information
  • Student information
  • Institution or campus
  • Year of study

Accommodation Information — if you search for, contact, or list a boarding house, we may collect:

  • Accommodation preferences
  • Property information
  • Location information
  • Booking information
  • Landlord information
  • Communications relating to accommodation

Marketplace Information — if you buy, sell, or advertise products, we may collect:

  • Product information
  • Listing photographs
  • Prices
  • Seller information
  • Buyer information
  • Transaction information

Service Information — when you request services such as assignment support, typing, printing, photocopying, or other services, we may collect information and documents necessary to provide those services.

Payment Information — where payments are supported, we may collect information necessary to process or confirm your payment. Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. Uniplug does not necessarily store complete card, mobile-money, or banking credentials.

Technical Information may include:

  • IP address
  • Device type
  • Browser type
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • Usage information
  • Log information
  • Approximate location

6Public Information

Information you voluntarily publish on Uniplug may be visible to other users.

For example, a boarding-house listing may display:

  • Property name

  • Photographs
  • Price
  • Amenities
  • Description
  • Availability

Marketplace listings and other information that you choose to publish may also be visible to other users.

Users should avoid publishing sensitive personal information that they do not want publicly available.
